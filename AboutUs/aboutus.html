關於我們

同翕設計是一群相信空間可以改變生活的人，一個由理念聚合的團隊，我們來自不同背景，卻有一個共通點「都相信空間可以成為人與人之間最溫柔的橋樑。」
我們不急著追求華麗與速度，而是願意花時間理解每個人的故事與需求。因為我們知道，真正貼近人的設計，來自於真誠的聆聽與細膩的轉化。
在同翕，我們把每一個案子，都當作一次與生活深刻對話的機會；
我們不只設計一個「家」，更在意如何讓空間承載關係、夢想與未來。

Themis Design is a team brought together by a shared belief: that space can transform life.
We come from different backgrounds, but we are united by one conviction—
“space can become the gentlest bridge between people.”
We are in no rush for extravagance or speed; instead, we take the time to understand each person's story and needs.
Because we know that truly human-centered design is born from sincere listening and thoughtful transformation.
At Themis, every project is an opportunity to have a deep conversation with life itself.
We don't just design a “home”; we care about how a space can hold relationships, dreams, and the future.
Our hope is to make design approachable, relatable, and lasting.
